Selected Reviewers

Selection policy: reviewer panels are constructed using semantic relevance, authority balancing, workload constraints, and complementary expertise diversification.
Formula: Final Score = Semantic × w_s + KGeffective × w_k + Authority × w_a + LLM expertise match × w_l + Workload availability × w_w.
KG Effective: KGeffective = KG similarity × KG confidence, so graph evidence contributes continuously while low-confidence evidence has less influence.
Panel Policy: selected reviewers must pass panel quality floors and improve the overall balance of relevance, authority, workload, and complementary topical coverage.
Confidence: Relevance confidence is used for interpretability and audit, not as a direct scoring term.
Scales: Semantic scores are min-max normalized across the candidate pool; final display uses calibrated percentages to avoid overclaiming certainty.
Reproducibility: fixed seeds and cached embeddings/assignments.
